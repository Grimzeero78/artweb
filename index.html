03 — The Artist

Obsessed with
layers.

Isaac Njuguna Bridget — 22 years old. He lives at the intersection of art, sport, and code. A stencil artist, a football player, and a software developer. Three pillars that define his rhythm: creativity, discipline, and logic.

Guided by faith — God, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit — Isaac approaches every canvas with precision and soul. His work is a meditation on structure, contrast, and the beauty found in layered complexity.

Every piece is original. No prints, no reproductions. When you collect his work, you own a fragment of his process — something that cannot be replicated.
